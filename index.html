COMPANY INTRODUCTION

10 Years of Expertise in the Vacuum Equipment Industry

Kunhong (Shandong) Vacuum Technology Co., Ltd. is located in Zhuyang Town Industrial Park, at the eastern foot of the world-renowned Mount Tai Scenic Area. This location not only boasts beautiful scenery but also gathers abundant industrial resources and convenient transportation advantages, providing unique conditions for the company's development.
Since its establishment, Kunhong Technology has always focused on innovation and manufacturing in the vacuum field and metal pressure vessel field, achieving remarkable results in the development of vacuum application equipment, vacuum acquisition equipment, non-standard vacuum equipment, and pressure vessel products. Through continuous investment and close cooperation with scientific research institutions, the company deeply explores new product performance and application potential, committed to providing high-performance, high-quality solutions. From design to production, Kunhong Technology strictly controls every step, providing customers with diversified and customized products to meet different needs, earning high recognition from users. Currently, the company's products are widely used in high-end fields such as aerospace, new energy vehicles, electric power, metallurgy, petrochemical, shipbuilding, military industry, providing strong support for the development of related industries.
